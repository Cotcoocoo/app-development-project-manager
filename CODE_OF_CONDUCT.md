# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Focus criticism on ideas, behavior, evidence, and project outcomes—not people.
- Use clear, respectful language and assume good intent while asking for clarification when needed.
- Respect different technical backgrounds, product contexts, and accessibility needs.
- Protect private information, including credentials, customer data, internal project material, and personal details.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes transparently.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private, sensitive, or identifying information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading contributors about evidence, security, release readiness, or project status.
- Spam, abusive language, or disruptive off-topic behavior.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the method in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate actions to protect the community.
